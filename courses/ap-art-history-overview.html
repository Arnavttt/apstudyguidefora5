About This Course

AP® Art History introduces students to the history of art across cultures and time periods, from prehistoric works to contemporary global art. Students analyze art through formal analysis, cultural context, and critical interpretation. The course emphasizes visual thinking and writing skills essential for understanding humanity's artistic heritage.

Key Skills Tested

  • Visual analysis of art objects, architecture, and artifacts
  • Contextual interpretation within cultural and historical frameworks
  • Comparative analysis across cultures and time periods
  • Using evidence-based argumentation in essay responses

Scoring Tip: The AP exam tests 250 works on the required image list. Memorize key details (artist, date, medium, patron, cultural context) for each. Short-answer questions require concise, specific evidence—avoid vague generalities.
