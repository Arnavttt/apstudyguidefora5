Exam Day Tips

✓

For FRQs, always identify the work by title, artist, date, and medium before analyzing.

✓

The short-answer questions often pair a required work with an unknown work—practice comparing.

✓

Don't skip works outside Western Europe; global works appear heavily on the exam.

✓

Manage time carefully: 80 MCQs in 60 min = 45 seconds each.
