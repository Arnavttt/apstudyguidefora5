About This Course

AP® Biology covers the foundational principles of living systems at scales from molecules to ecosystems. The course is built around four big ideas: evolution, cellular processes, information storage and transmission, and interactions within biological systems. Students develop scientific inquiry skills through laboratory investigations.

Key Skills Tested

  • Modeling and representing biological systems
  • Using quantitative reasoning and data analysis
  • Constructing and supporting scientific claims with evidence
  • Applying concepts across multiple scales of biological organization

Scoring Tip: AP Bio FRQs reward thorough mechanistic explanations. For any 'explain' prompt, describe the step-by-step mechanism, not just the outcome. Graph interpretation questions require you to read axis labels precisely and describe trends with specific numbers.
