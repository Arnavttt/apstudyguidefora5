Exam Day Tips

✓

Know the four big ideas and the science practices—FRQ scoring rubrics reference them.

✓

For genetics problems, draw Punnett squares or pedigrees before writing your answer.

✓

Graph questions: always label axes, include units, and plot points accurately.

✓

Memorize the Chi-square formula and know when to apply it for goodness-of-fit.
