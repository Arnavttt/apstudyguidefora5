About This Course

AP® Calculus AB is a full year of single-variable calculus covering limits and continuity, differentiation, integration and the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus, differential equations, and applications of derivatives and integrals. It is roughly the first semester of college calculus and emphasizes conceptual understanding alongside procedural fluency. Use the adaptive practice below to build mastery topic by topic.

Key Skills Tested

  • Evaluating limits and analyzing continuity
  • Computing derivatives and applying them to motion, related rates, and optimization
  • Evaluating definite and indefinite integrals and using the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus
  • Justifying conclusions with clear mathematical reasoning

Scoring Tip: On FRQs, set up the problem with correct notation before computing — you earn setup points even if arithmetic slips. Show the antiderivative and evaluation steps explicitly, and label units in applied problems.
