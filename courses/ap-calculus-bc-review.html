Exam Day Tips

✓

Show all work on FRQs—unsupported answers receive no credit even if correct.

✓

Know when to use each convergence test (ratio, comparison, integral, alternating series).

✓

For Taylor series, memorize the standard series for e^x, sin x, cos x, 1/(1−x).

✓

Units matter in applied problems—include them in every answer that involves real-world context.
