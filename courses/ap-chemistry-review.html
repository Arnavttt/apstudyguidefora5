Essential Concepts

  • Periodic trends: atomic radius, ionization energy, electronegativity
  • VSEPR theory and molecular geometry
  • Thermodynamics: ΔH, ΔS, ΔG, and spontaneity
  • Kinetics: rate laws, half-life, Arrhenius equation
  • Acid-base equilibria: Ka, Kb, buffers, titrations

Exam Day Tips

✓

Memorize solubility rules, strong acids/bases, and activity series.

✓

For equilibrium problems, use an ICE table and check your assumptions.

✓

Lab-based questions appear on both sections—understand common procedures like titration.

✓

The photoelectron spectroscopy (PES) question appears almost every year.
