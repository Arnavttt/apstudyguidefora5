About This Course

AP® Comparative Government and Politics examines the political systems of six core countries: the United Kingdom, Mexico, Russia, China, Iran, and Nigeria. Students compare governmental structures, political institutions, regime types, and the relationship between citizens and the state across democratic and authoritarian systems.

Key Skills Tested

  • Comparing political institutions across the six core countries
  • Defining and applying political science concepts
  • Constructing evidence-based comparative arguments
  • Analyzing data (charts, maps, tables) in a political context

Scoring Tip: The argument essay rewards specific country examples and conceptual vocabulary. Use the correct political science terminology throughout. For FRQ comparisons, always identify which countries you are comparing at the start.
