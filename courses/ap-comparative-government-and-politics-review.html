Exam Day Tips

✓

Know one or two specific examples for each of the six countries on every major topic.

✓

The argument essay must include a defensible thesis, evidence, and a counter-argument.

✓

Data questions often test whether a country's data matches what you know—trust your knowledge.

✓

Don't confuse UK and US systems: UK is parliamentary with parliamentary sovereignty.
