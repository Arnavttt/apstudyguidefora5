About This Course

AP® Computer Science Principles introduces the foundational ideas of computer science, including creative development, data analysis, algorithms, programming, the internet, and the impacts of computing. Students complete a Create Performance Task portfolio project and demonstrate understanding of computing's societal impacts.

Key Skills Tested

  • Designing and implementing algorithms in a block or text-based language
  • Analyzing data to identify patterns and draw conclusions
  • Evaluating the societal, ethical, and security impacts of computing
  • Explaining abstraction and its role in program design

Scoring Tip: For the Create PT, the written responses are scored by rubric—address each required element explicitly. Your video must show your program running with the required functionality. On the MCQ, read carefully: 'which of the following is NOT' questions trip up many students.
