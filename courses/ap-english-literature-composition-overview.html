About This Course

AP® English Literature and Composition develops students' ability to read and analyze complex literary works—fiction, poetry, and drama—from various time periods and cultures. Students learn to write analytical essays that interpret literature using close reading, figurative language, structure, and thematic analysis.

Key Skills Tested

  • Close reading and interpreting literary texts at the word, sentence, and structural level
  • Analyzing how literary devices create meaning and effect
  • Writing evidence-based literary analysis essays
  • Identifying themes and character development across literary works

Scoring Tip: Essays are scored on a 0–6 rubric. Anchor your thesis to a specific literary interpretation, not a plot summary. For Q3 (open essay), choose a work you know deeply—avoid vague novels. Quote precisely and briefly; your commentary should be longer than your quotations.
