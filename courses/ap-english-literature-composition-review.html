Exam Day Tips

✓

Read every poem and prose passage twice before answering MCQs—once for gist, once for detail.

✓

For essays, avoid plot summary—every sentence should make an interpretive claim.

✓

Q3 open essay: plan two or three specific scenes/passages you can cite from memory.

✓

Tone words matter: practice distinguishing 'sardonic' from 'ironic' from 'satirical.'
