Exam Day Tips

✓

Know key environmental laws: Clean Air Act, Clean Water Act, CERCLA, ESA, NEPA.

✓

Practice math problems: half-life, energy conversions, population growth (Rule of 70).

✓

FRQ part D often asks you to describe an environmental law or regulation—be specific.

✓

Memorize the IPAT equation: I = P × A × T (Impact = Population × Affluence × Technology).
