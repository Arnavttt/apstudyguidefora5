About This Course

AP® European History traces the political, cultural, economic, and social development of Europe from approximately 1450 to the present. Students examine the Renaissance, Reformation, Scientific Revolution, Enlightenment, industrialization, world wars, and contemporary Europe, developing skills in historical analysis, causation, and argumentation.

Key Skills Tested

  • Analyzing primary and secondary source documents (HAPP-Y: historical situation, audience, purpose, point of view, explain why)
  • Writing historically-argued DBQs and LEQs with thesis, evidence, and complexity
  • Applying historical thinking skills: causation, continuity/change, comparison, contextualization
  • Tracing long-term developments and turning points across European history

Scoring Tip: DBQ scoring rewards using at least 6 of 7 documents with sourcing on 3+. Complexity point: often easiest earned via 'throughout the period' or 'cause AND effect' framing. SAQs don't require a thesis—just answer each part directly with evidence.
