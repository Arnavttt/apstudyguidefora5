Essential Concepts

  • Renaissance and Reformation: challenge to Church authority
  • Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment: reason over tradition
  • French Revolution: causes, radical phase, Napoleonic legacy
  • 19th-century -isms: nationalism, liberalism, conservatism, socialism
  • World Wars and the reshaping of European borders and societies

Exam Day Tips

✓

For every document in the DBQ, identify: historical situation, audience, purpose, AND point of view.

✓

LEQ must include contextualization (a full paragraph of relevant background before your thesis).

✓

Memorize key dates: 1517, 1648, 1789, 1815, 1848, 1871, 1914, 1919, 1939, 1945, 1989.

✓

Avoid 'the document says'—instead analyze why the author wrote what they did.
