About This Course

AP® Human Geography introduces students to the systematic study of patterns and processes that shape human understanding and use of Earth's surface. Topics include population, migration, culture, political geography, agriculture, industrialization, urbanization, and globalization, all examined through geographic methods and spatial analysis.

Key Skills Tested

  • Using geographic concepts and models to interpret data and maps
  • Applying spatial reasoning to human patterns and processes
  • Analyzing how physical environment influences and is influenced by human activity
  • Writing evidence-based geographic arguments

Scoring Tip: FRQ parts are worth 1–3 points each—answer them in the order given and label each part (A, B, C). Define geographic terms before applying them—a definition earns a point in most rubrics. Use specific place examples whenever the prompt says 'support with evidence.'
