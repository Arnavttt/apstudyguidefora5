Essential Concepts

  • Population distribution and the demographic transition model
  • Migration patterns: voluntary, forced, internal, international
  • Cultural diffusion: relocation, expansion, hierarchical, contagious
  • Political geography: states, nations, boundaries, supranationalism
  • Urban models: concentric zone, sector, multiple nuclei, galactic city

Exam Day Tips

✓

Know all major geographic models: Demographic Transition, Von Thünen, Christaller, Rostow, Wallerstein.

✓

For any map-based question, identify the spatial pattern and explain its cause.

✓

FRQ part C often asks for consequences or solutions—always justify your answer.

✓

Distinguish between similar terms: ethnicity vs. nationality, state vs. nation vs. nation-state.
