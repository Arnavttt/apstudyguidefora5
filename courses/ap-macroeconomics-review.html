Essential Concepts

  • Measuring GDP: expenditure approach (C + I + G + NX)
  • Unemployment types: frictional, structural, cyclical, natural rate
  • Inflation: demand-pull, cost-push, and the quantity theory of money
  • Fiscal policy: multiplier effect, crowding out, automatic stabilizers
  • Monetary policy: Fed tools, money supply, interest rates

Exam Day Tips

✓

Know which direction each curve shifts for every type of shock or policy.

✓

The long FRQ always involves drawing several connected graphs—practice graph sequences.

✓

For open economy questions, know how interest rate changes affect the exchange rate and NX.

✓

Don't confuse the money market (nominal interest rate) with loanable funds (real interest rate).
