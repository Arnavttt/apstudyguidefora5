About This Course

AP® Microeconomics examines how individual households and firms make decisions, and how these interact in markets. Topics include supply and demand, elasticity, consumer theory, production costs, market structures (perfect competition, monopoly, oligopoly), and market failures. Students analyze welfare economics, externalities, and public goods.

Key Skills Tested

  • Drawing and interpreting microeconomic graphs (supply/demand, cost curves, MR/MC)
  • Applying marginal analysis to optimize firm and consumer decisions
  • Identifying market failures and evaluating government interventions
  • Comparing outcomes across market structures

Scoring Tip: Microeconomics FRQs heavily involve graphs—label every curve, axis, equilibrium point, and shaded area. For profit/loss questions, shade DWL or profit area precisely. Show your work on calculations (TR, TC, profit) even when just multiplying.
