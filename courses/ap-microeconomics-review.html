Essential Concepts

  • Supply and demand: determinants of shifts vs. movements along
  • Elasticity: price elasticity of demand/supply, income, cross-price
  • Production and costs: fixed, variable, MC, ATC, AVC, AFC
  • Market structures: PC, monopolistic competition, oligopoly, monopoly
  • Factor markets: labor demand/supply, wage determination

Exam Day Tips

✓

Profit-maximizing rule is always MR = MC—applicable to all market structures.

✓

Know shutdown condition (P < AVC) vs. exit condition (P < ATC) in the long run.

✓

For externality questions, know the socially optimal output and how to achieve it.

✓

Oligopoly questions often involve game theory—know dominant strategy and Nash equilibrium.
