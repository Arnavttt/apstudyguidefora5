About This Course

AP® Music Theory develops students' ability to read, write, and analyze music. Topics include pitch notation, rhythm and meter, scales, intervals, chords, four-part voice leading, harmonic analysis, musical form, and ear training (sight-singing and melodic dictation). The course bridges theoretical knowledge with practical musical performance.

Key Skills Tested

  • Reading and writing music notation (treble, bass, alto, tenor clef)
  • Identifying and constructing intervals, scales, chords, and progressions
  • Harmonizing a melody using four-part SATB voice leading rules
  • Recognizing musical forms and harmonic functions by ear

Scoring Tip: Sight-singing accounts for 10% of your score—practice daily with a pitch reference. Four-part writing FRQs: avoid parallel fifths/octaves and keep proper voice ranges. Melodic dictation: use the rhythm first, then fill in pitches.
