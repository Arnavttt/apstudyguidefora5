Essential Concepts

  • Major and minor scales: construction, key signatures, relative/parallel
  • Intervals: identification, inversion, compound intervals
  • Triads and seventh chords in root position and inversions
  • Four-part voice leading: doubling rules, spacing, voice crossing, parallels
  • Harmonic function: tonic, subdominant, dominant; functional progressions

Exam Day Tips

✓

Memorize all key signatures (up to 7 sharps/flats) instantly—it saves time on every question.

✓

For harmonic analysis, identify the key first, then label each chord with Roman numerals.

✓

Non-chord tones appear heavily in MCQ aural questions—know PT, NT, SUS, APP, ANT, ET.

✓

Sight-sing everything you practice with solfège or scale degrees, not just letter names.
