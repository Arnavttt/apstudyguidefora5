Exam Day Tips

✓

Sketching free-body diagrams before writing equations earns points and prevents errors.

✓

For multi-part FRQs, incorrect answers in part (a) don't penalize correct work in part (b).

✓

Know which conservation laws apply (energy, momentum, charge) to each scenario.

✓

Read multi-select MCQs carefully—you must choose ALL correct answers for full credit.
