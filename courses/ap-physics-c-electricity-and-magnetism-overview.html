About This Course

AP® Physics C: Electricity and Magnetism is a calculus-based course covering electric fields and potential, Gauss's law, capacitance, DC circuits, magnetic fields, electromagnetic induction, and inductance. It corresponds to the second semester of introductory college physics and requires concurrent enrollment in or completion of calculus.

Key Skills Tested

  • Applying Gauss's law and Ampere's law to symmetric charge/current distributions
  • Setting up and solving differential equations for circuits and electromagnetic induction
  • Using calculus (integration and differentiation) to derive electromagnetic quantities
  • Interpreting graphs of E, V, B, and flux as functions of position

Scoring Tip: FRQs explicitly require calculus—use integrals to find E, V, or flux whenever the charge distribution is non-trivial. Show Gaussian surface setup clearly before applying Gauss's law. RC circuit problems: write the differential equation, then solve it—don't skip steps.
