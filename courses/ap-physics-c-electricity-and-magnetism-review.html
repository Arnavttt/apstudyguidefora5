Essential Concepts

  • Electric force and field: Coulomb's law, superposition
  • Gauss's law: applying to spheres, cylinders, planes
  • Potential energy, potential, and potential difference
  • Capacitors: parallel/series combinations, energy stored, dielectrics
  • Magnetic induction: Faraday's/Lenz's law, motional EMF, RL circuits

Exam Day Tips

✓

Gauss's law: choose the right surface geometry (sphere for point/sphere, cylinder for line, plane for sheet).

✓

RC circuits: know exponential decay form V(t) = V₀e^(−t/RC) and what time constant τ means.

✓

Derivations of E inside and outside charged conductors appear frequently.

✓

For FRQs, points are awarded for setup, equation, and final answer separately.
