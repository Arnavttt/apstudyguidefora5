About This Course

AP® Physics C: Mechanics is a calculus-based course covering kinematics, Newton's laws, work-energy, oscillations, gravitation, and rotational motion. It corresponds to the first semester of introductory college physics and is designed for students with concurrent or prior calculus experience.

Key Skills Tested

  • Applying calculus to kinematics (derivatives and integrals of position, velocity, acceleration)
  • Solving dynamics problems using Newton's laws and free-body diagrams
  • Applying conservation of energy and momentum to complex systems
  • Analyzing rotational dynamics including torque, inertia, and angular momentum

Scoring Tip: Use calculus explicitly—FRQ rubrics reward integration/differentiation steps. For rotation problems, draw the system with all torques labeled before writing equations. Energy methods are often faster than force methods for complex trajectories—use both strategically.
