Essential Concepts

  • Kinematics: equations of motion, calculus forms (v = dx/dt, a = dv/dt)
  • Newton's laws: force analysis, constraint problems (pulleys, inclines)
  • Work and energy: work-energy theorem, conservation of mechanical energy
  • Impulse and momentum: collisions (elastic, inelastic), rocket propulsion
  • Rotation: rolling without slipping, parallel axis theorem, precession

Exam Day Tips

✓

FRQ setup: draw a diagram, define a coordinate system, and write your equation of motion before solving.

✓

Simple harmonic motion: know ω² = k/m for spring and ω² = g/L for pendulum.

✓

Rolling without slipping: v_cm = rω and a_cm = rα—these links always hold.

✓

Memorize standard moments of inertia: solid sphere (2/5 MR²), hoop (MR²), solid disk (1/2 MR²).
