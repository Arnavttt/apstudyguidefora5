Exam Day Tips

✓

Know the six trig functions on the unit circle at all standard angles instantly.

✓

For exponential models: y = ab^t vs. y = ae^(kt)—understand the difference and when each is used.

✓

Composition problems: f(g(x)) requires the range of g to be in the domain of f—check this.

✓

Transformations: know the order (horizontal first inside the function, then vertical outside).
