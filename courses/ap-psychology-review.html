Essential Concepts

  • Biological bases: neurons, neurotransmitters, brain structures, genetics
  • Learning: classical conditioning (Pavlov), operant conditioning (Skinner), observational (Bandura)
  • Memory: encoding, storage, retrieval; types of long-term memory
  • Psychological disorders: diagnosis, classification, treatment approaches
  • Social psychology: conformity, obedience, attribution, group dynamics

Exam Day Tips

✓

Memorize key researchers and their associated concepts (Pavlov, Skinner, Bandura, Freud, Kohlberg, Piaget).

✓

For FRQs, practice writing one sentence per concept: define → apply → explain effect.

✓

Know the difference between correlation and causation—appears on nearly every exam.

✓

Research design questions: know independent variable, dependent variable, operational definition, placebo effect.
