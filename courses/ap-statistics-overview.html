About This Course

AP® Statistics introduces the major concepts and tools for collecting, analyzing, and drawing conclusions from data. The four themes are exploring data, sampling and experimentation, probability and simulation, and statistical inference (confidence intervals and significance tests). A graphing calculator is expected throughout. Use the adaptive practice below to build mastery topic by topic.

Key Skills Tested

  • Describing distributions and interpreting graphs, center, and spread
  • Designing samples and experiments and identifying sources of bias
  • Applying probability rules and random-variable distributions
  • Constructing confidence intervals and carrying out significance tests, then interpreting results in context

Scoring Tip: On inference FRQs, always state the procedure, check conditions, show the mechanics, and write a conclusion in context that links back to the significance level. Interpretations that omit context lose credit.
