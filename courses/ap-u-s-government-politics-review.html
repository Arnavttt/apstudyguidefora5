Essential Concepts

  • Constitutional foundations: Articles I–III, Bill of Rights, amendments
  • Federal vs. state power: categorical grants, block grants, unfunded mandates
  • Congress: structure, powers, legislative process, incumbency
  • The Presidency: formal/informal powers, Executive Office, veto power
  • The Supreme Court: jurisdiction, judicial review, landmark cases

Exam Day Tips

✓

Know all 15 required SCOTUS cases: holding, constitutional basis, significance.

✓

Know all 9 required documents: Federalist 10, 51, 70, 78; Declaration; Constitution; Letter from Birmingham Jail; others.

✓

For the argument essay, use specific case names and document titles—not just 'the Founders said.'

✓

Quantitative FRQ: always describe the data first (what does it show?), then analyze it.
