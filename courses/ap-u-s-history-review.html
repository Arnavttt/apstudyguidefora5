Essential Concepts

  • Colonial period: Chesapeake vs. New England colonies, slavery's origins
  • Revolutionary era: Enlightenment ideas, Articles of Confederation, Constitution
  • Antebellum period: Jacksonian democracy, slavery, reform movements, sectionalism
  • Civil War and Reconstruction: causes, conduct, legacy, failure of Reconstruction
  • 20th century: Progressive Era reforms, New Deal, WWII, Cold War, Civil Rights, Reagan Revolution

Exam Day Tips

✓

For DBQ sourcing, ask: Who wrote it? For what audience? For what purpose? How does their position affect the content?

✓

Know major turning points: 1607, 1763, 1787, 1820, 1848, 1877, 1898, 1919, 1945, 1968, 1980.

✓

LEQ: contextualization paragraph must go before your thesis and be at least 5–7 sentences.

✓

APUSH MCQs almost always include a primary source stimulus—analyze it before reading the questions.
