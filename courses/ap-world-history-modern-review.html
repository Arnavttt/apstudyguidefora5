Essential Concepts

  • Afro-Eurasian trade networks c. 1200–1450: Mongols, Silk Road, Indian Ocean
  • Age of Exploration: European expansion, Columbian Exchange, plantation economies
  • Revolutions: American, French, Haitian, Latin American independence movements
  • 19th-century industrialization and imperialism in Africa, Asia, and the Americas
  • 20th-century decolonization, Cold War, and post-Cold War globalization

Exam Day Tips

✓

Use the HAPP-Y framework for every document: Historical situation, Audience, Purpose, Point of view, explain Why it matters.

✓

LEQ time periods: c.1200–1450, 1450–1750, 1750–1900, 1900–present—know the boundaries.

✓

For complexity, try comparing world regions rather than just one country's history.

✓

Avoid Eurocentrism: include Asia, Africa, and the Americas in every essay you write.
