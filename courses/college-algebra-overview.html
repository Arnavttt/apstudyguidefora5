About This Course

College Algebra covers the foundational algebraic skills needed for college-level mathematics. Topics include linear and quadratic equations, systems of equations, polynomial and rational functions, exponential and logarithmic functions, and conic sections. The course emphasizes algebraic reasoning, graphing, and real-world applications.

Key Skills Tested

  • Solving linear, quadratic, polynomial, and rational equations
  • Graphing functions and interpreting their behavior
  • Working with exponential and logarithmic expressions
  • Setting up and solving systems of equations

Scoring Tip: Show all algebraic steps—partial credit is awarded for correct setup even if you make an arithmetic error. For word problems, define variables before writing equations. Double-check solutions by substituting back into the original equation.
