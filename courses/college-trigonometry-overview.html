About This Course

College Trigonometry develops a deep understanding of the six trigonometric functions and their applications. Topics include the unit circle, radian and degree measure, trigonometric equations and identities, inverse trigonometric functions, triangles (laws of sines and cosines), polar coordinates, and vectors. Applications include modeling periodic phenomena and solving geometric problems.

Key Skills Tested

  • Evaluating all six trig functions at standard angles using the unit circle
  • Graphing sinusoidal functions with transformations (amplitude, period, phase shift)
  • Proving and applying trigonometric identities
  • Solving triangles using the Law of Sines and Law of Cosines

Scoring Tip: Know the unit circle values cold—they are the foundation of every trig problem. For identity proofs, work on one side only; substituting Pythagorean identities is almost always the first step. In ambiguous case (Law of Sines), always check if two triangles are possible.
