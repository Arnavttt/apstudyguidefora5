Essential Concepts

  • Unit circle: coordinates, standard angle values (0°, 30°, 45°, 60°, 90°, and reflections)
  • Graphing sin, cos, tan: period = 2π/B, amplitude = |A|, phase shift = −C/B
  • Fundamental identities: Pythagorean, quotient, reciprocal
  • Sum/difference and double-angle formulas
  • Laws of Sines and Cosines for oblique triangles

Exam Day Tips

✓

Reference angle is always positive and between 0° and 90°—use it to evaluate trig in any quadrant.

✓

For graphing: identify A, B, C, D in y = A·sin(Bx − C) + D before sketching.

✓

Inverse trig: arcsin and arccos have restricted domains—know them ([−π/2, π/2] and [0, π]).

✓

Polar conversion: x = r·cos θ, y = r·sin θ, r² = x² + y².
